## How I'll use this register

I will re-read this register at every 10:00 check-in and again at the 16:00 wrap, and update it whenever a check turns red or a new surprise appears. It lives in the team repo, so the project sponsor, the three engineering teams, and my pair or peers can all see it, and I will share it with the sponsor at each 15:30 check-in. Each risk has a named owner and an escalation trigger, so when a trigger fires I route the problem to that team instead of fixing it myself.
